B.

Search ko buttons ki jagah lena chahiye, unke saath nahi — warna hero me teen competing actions ho jate hain aur koi bhi jeet nahi paata. A aur B dono ye karte hain; B better hai kyunki khaali search box ke saamne log soch me pad jate hain ("kya likhun — college ka naam? course? city?"). Chips wo sawaal khatam kar dete hain, aur saath hi ye bhi bata dete hain ki catalogue me hai kya. Puck button navbar wale CTA se match bhi karta hai.

C tab lo agar assessment ko hero me hi rakhna zaroori lage — par wo line ("Pata nahi kya dhoondhna hai?") search se dhyan hata deti hai. D sabse safe hai, hero bilkul waisa ka waisa, par search stats card se pehle ek aur horizontal band daal deta hai — page bhaari lagne lagta hai.

Ek cheez jo A/B/C teeno me hai: "Explore Colleges" button khatam ho jata hai. Wo theek hai — search wahi kaam behtar karta hai, aur wo button waise bhi sirf /colleges pe le jata tha.
