Meri raay

02 ya 04. Abhi ka problem ye hai ki navbar khud ek pill hai aur uske andar ek aur pill baitha hai — do concentric pills, isliye button "chipka hua" lagta hai. Rectangle (12px radius) us tension ko todta hai, aur site ke baaki saare buttons pehle se 12–16px radius pe hain, toh consistency bhi aa jati hai. 04 wahi hai plus arrow, jo isse baaki nav links se alag "action" jaisa banata hai.

Agar pill hi rakhna hai to 03 — same shape, bas saans lene ki jagah zyada. 09 notebook direction se sabse zyada match karta hai par pure site pe wo treatment repeat karna padega, sirf navbar me akela ajeeb lagega.
