Meri raay

03 (hata do) ya 07 (phone number).

Asli baat ye hai ki ek admission consultancy ke liye "Contact" aur "Free Counselling" lagbhag ek hi cheez hai — dono ka matlab hai "kisi insaan se baat karo". Do alag-alag entry points dene se visitor ek second ruk jata hai, aur wo ruk-na hi tumhe ajeeb lag raha hai. Shape ki problem baad me hai, duplication pehle.

03 sabse saaf hai — CTA hi contact ka kaam kar deta hai, aur address/phone/email footer me pehle se poora hai. 07 tab lo agar phone number upar dikhana zaroori lage: ye duplicate nahi hai (ye turant call hai, jabki CTA form hai), aur Indian education market me visible number trust kaafi badhata hai.

Agar shabd "Contact" hi rakhna hai to 04 — hairline divider bina kuch bole bata deta hai ki ye alag group hai. 02 se bhi kaam chalega par tab nav 7 items ka ho jayega, thoda bhara-bhara.
